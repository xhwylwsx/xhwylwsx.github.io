I am currently an assistant professor in [School of Statistics and Data Science](https://ssds.sufe.edu.cn/main.htm) at [Shanghai University of Finance and Economics](https://www.sufe.edu.cn/). I got my Ph.D. degree in Statistics from [School of Statistics and Data Science, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics](https://ssds.sufe.edu.cn/main.htm) in 2022, under the supervision of [Prof. Jinhong You](https://ssds.sufe.edu.cn/f7/82/c713a128898/page.htm). During 2019.08-2020.04 and 2020.10-2021.09, I studied as a Visiting Research Student in [Department of Mathematics, Hong Kong Baptist University](https://www.math.hkbu.edu.hk/v1/) under the supervision of [Prof. Ming-Yen Cheng](https://www.math.hkbu.edu.hk/~chengmingyen/). During 2022-2024, I worked as a Postdoc Fellow in  [School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University](https://www.math.pku.edu.cn/) with [Prof. Song Xi Chen](https://www.math.pku.edu.cn/). My research interests are mainly on functional data analysis, non-parametric/semi-parametric modelling and inference, data fusion and data assimilation, interdisciplinary studies on atmospheric science, geoscience, oceanography and statistics.
